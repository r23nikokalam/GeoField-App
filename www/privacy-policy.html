GeoField Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026
Application: GeoField — Geological Field Observation Notebook
Developer name used in the Google Play listing: GeoField
Privacy and support contact: R23Nikokalam@gmail.com

1. Summary

GeoField is designed to work offline. The app stores geological field records locally on the user's Android device. The app does not require an account and does not transmit observation records to a GeoField-operated server.

2. Information processed by the app

Users may enter project details, geologist names, coordinates, geological descriptions, structural measurements, sample identifiers, and other field-notebook information. This information is processed locally to provide the app's notebook, editing, Excel export, and JSON backup/restore features.

On Android, the app encrypts its local record store using AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is held by Android Keystore. Availability of hardware-backed key protection depends on the device.

3. Collection, transmission, and sharing

GeoField does not include account registration, advertising, analytics, or a GeoField-operated cloud synchronization service. The app is configured without the Android Internet permission and is designed not to send field records or device information to the developer or to third-party servers.

When a user explicitly exports an XLSX or JSON file, GeoField creates the file locally and invokes the Android share interface. The user chooses whether and where to save or share that file. Any recipient app or service selected by the user is governed by that provider's own privacy practices.

4. Backups and security

The in-app record store is encrypted on Android. User-created JSON backups are portable plaintext files and are not encrypted by GeoField. Anyone with access to such a backup may be able to read its contents. Users should store backup files securely and share them only with trusted recipients.

Android system backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for GeoField's app data because the encryption key is device-bound. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove the encrypted records and key. Users should export and verify a backup before uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free. Users should protect their devices with an appropriate screen lock and keep their operating system up to date.

5. Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until the user deletes individual records, uses the app's Delete All function, clears app data, or uninstalls the app. GeoField does not maintain a separate server-side copy of those records.

Exported files are stored or shared through locations and services selected by the user. The user is responsible for managing and deleting those copies.

6. Children's privacy

GeoField is a professional field-notebook utility and is not designed to target children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy is published.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the GeoField developer at R23Nikokalam@gmail.com.
